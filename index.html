Arrival

Check-In Instructions

We're excited to host you and hope you enjoy your stay! Below are the check-in instructions and details to ensure a seamless arrival.

1

Parking

Driveway, and street parking is first-come, first-served!

2

Front door code

Your unique door code will be available in your SuiteOp link after you have completed all required steps in the guest portal.

3

Using the lock

When unlocking, wait for the lock to whirr three times and stop before turning it. Turning the lock too soon will cause the unlock to fail. Multiple failed attempts may lock you out. Pull the door knob towards you to align the lock with the hole, and wait for the lock to whirr three times and stop before turning it.

4

Housekeeping recommendations

Shoes off policy, or use indoor shoes only. Indoor house slippers have been provided for your convenience during your stay. Please use the shoe rack located at the front entrance and remove your shoes to help maintain the cleanliness of the home.

Backyard

Pool Instructions

Pool Cover

  • Put the cover on before and after each use — the property has mature trees and pollen moves fast.
  • Two hooks just behind the pool — one on the left post, one on the right — for hanging the cover when it's off.
  • In case of heavy rain, secure the cover tightly and tie both drawstrings on each end.

Skimming

  • If you spot leaves or debris floating on top, grab the skimmer and do a quick skim.

Pool Pump Maintenance

  • The pump and filter are all set — no adjustments needed.
  • If anything looks off, just send us a message and we'll handle it right away.

Before you leave

Check-Out

Checkout is at 10:00 AM. If we haven't discussed a late checkout, please plan to leave by then, as our cleaning team will arrive promptly.

  • Load and run the dishwasher with any used dishes. Do not use the heat-dry function.
  • Place all towels in the washer, add 1 Tide pod, and run on a cold wash. Tossing them in the dryer afterward is a bonus and greatly appreciated!
  • Throw away all trash, including any refrigerated items. Bag everything completely and place extras in the outdoor bin. Unbagged trash may result in an additional fee.
  • Gather all belongings. Items left behind will not be shipped and will be discarded.
  • Return any moved furniture, cornhole bags, chairs, and backyard décor to their original positions.
  • Close and lock all windows and doors.
  • Turn off all lights and appliances.
  • Return all amenities to their proper locations: ping pong paddles/balls, mini golf equipment, soccer gear, etc.
  • Pool: place the cover on the pool and ensure the cover is tightened using the side drawstrings (2 of them).
  • Firepit: ensure the fire is completely out before leaving.
  • Dispose of any pet waste if applicable — failure to do so will result in an additional fee.
  • Clean the grill if it was used — failure to do so will result in an additional fee.
